Privacy Policy for Split Cheers

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how getti nexa ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Split Cheers mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Split Cheers, we may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Nickname
  • Hometown
  • Account authentication information
  • Event or trip information
  • Participant information
  • Expense information
  • Information about who paid an expense and how expenses are shared

2. Phone Number and User Search

Your phone number is used as an identifier for signing in to your Split Cheers account.

Your phone number may also be used to help other Split Cheers users search for and identify friends or participants when using features that involve adding people to events or expense groups.

3. Event and Expense Information

Split Cheers collects information that you provide when creating or participating in events and recording expenses.

This may include:

  • Event or trip names
  • Places
  • Dates
  • Participants
  • Expense descriptions
  • Expense amounts
  • Information about who paid
  • Expense-sharing and settlement information

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your account
  • Allow users to find friends or participants
  • Create and manage events
  • Manage participants
  • Record and track expenses
  • Calculate and display expense sharing
  • Synchronize information between your device and our services
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Protect the security of the application
  • Provide customer support

5. Data Storage

Information associated with your account and application activity may be stored on backend infrastructure and databases used to operate Split Cheers.

Certain information may also be stored locally on your device to support application functionality, caching, or offline use.

6. Authentication and Device Storage

Authentication tokens or related credentials may be stored securely on your device to help maintain your signed-in session.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Certain profile information may be visible to other Split Cheers users when necessary for features such as finding friends, identifying participants, or managing shared expenses.

We may use third-party service providers for services such as application hosting, database infrastructure, authentication, security, or other technical services required to operate Split Cheers. These providers may process information on our behalf.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of users or the application.

8. Advertising

Split Cheers does not currently display advertisements.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Account Deletion

Split Cheers provides an option to delete your account from within the application.

If you cannot access the application and need assistance with account deletion, you may contact us using the email address provided below.

Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
